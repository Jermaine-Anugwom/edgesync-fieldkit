# EdgeSync FieldKit product brief

## Mode
Operate. The operator must recognize state, evidence, exceptions, and next safe action within seconds.

## User and task
A field-operations lead watches devices move sealed inspection records through unreliable links, identifies a sequence collision, and chooses the next safe recovery action without overwriting evidence.

## Non-negotiables
Synthetic data only; deterministic offline baseline; no destructive auto-merge; visible queue and conflict lineage; keyboard and mobile access; no model credential required.

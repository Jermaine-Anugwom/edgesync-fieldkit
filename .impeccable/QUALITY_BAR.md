# Quality bar

The approved build must make synchronization tension spatial: devices, route condition, packet history, conflict lineage, and safe recovery remain understandable in one view. A generic activity list or silent auto-merge fails the bar.

Reference challenger: [Tensegrity Breathing Column](https://impeccable.style/worlds/cards/kinetic-sculpture-automata-tensegrity-breathing-column.webp) and its [hero](https://impeccable.style/worlds/cards/kinetic-sculpture-automata-tensegrity-breathing-column-hero.webp). The commitment is visible tension and structure, not copied styling.
